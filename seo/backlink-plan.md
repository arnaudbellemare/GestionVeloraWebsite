## Backlink Principles

- Prioritize local relevance over raw domain count: Quebec property, condo, rental, legal, accounting, cleaning, maintenance, and real estate sites matter more than generic directories.
- Use real business relationships first: partners, vendors, associations, guest education, interviews, and local citations.
- Avoid paid link farms, irrelevant guest-post networks, spun directory submissions, and repeated exact-match anchors.
- Every backlink target should point to the page that best matches the referring context.
- Track links monthly in Google Search Console and record the target page, anchor text, referring URL, and status.

## Anchor Text Mix

Use a natural mix instead of repeating the same keyword.

| Anchor Type | Examples | Target Share |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | Gestion Velora | 40% |
| URL / naked | gestionvelora.com | 20% |
| Partial keyword | gestion de copropriete a Montreal, gestionnaire de copropriete, gestion locative a Montreal | 25% |
| Contextual / article title | guide sur le fonds de prevoyance, conseils pour syndicats de copropriete | 15% |

Exact-match anchors like `gestion de copropriete Montreal` should be rare and only used where they read naturally.

## Outreach Targets

1. Partner and vendor profiles
   - Cleaning, maintenance, construction, inspection, insurance, legal, accounting, and bookkeeping partners.
   - Best destination: `/services/syndicat-copropriete` for condo partners, `/services/location` for rental partners, `/services/airbnb` for short-term rental partners.

2. Association and member profiles
   - Relevant Quebec condo, property management, business, and local chamber profiles.
   - Use brand or URL anchors first; these are citation trust signals more than keyword pages.

3. Educational guest content
   - Topics: Loi 141, fonds de prevoyance, AGA preparation, condo board obligations, reserve studies, maintenance planning, rental screening, Airbnb compliance.
   - Link article body contextually to the matching service page or supporting blog article.

4. Local citations
   - Use complete NAP consistency: Gestion Velora, Montreal area service coverage, website, phone/email, service categories.
   - Keep only reputable local or industry directories; skip thin generic listing sites.

5. Partner case studies
   - Publish joint examples with vendors or professional partners when there is a legitimate project story.
   - Link to `/services/syndicat-copropriete` for condo-board operations and governance work.
